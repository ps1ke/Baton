baton

You are invited to help a project

Someone wants your machine in their project. With baton, your machine offers the AI models you allow, and the project's network gives it work: building, testing, reviewing. Nothing runs until you press Start, and nothing on your machine is shared except what you choose.

This link does not carry an invite. Ask for a new one: it looks like …/join.html#baton://…

1 · install

Install baton, if you have not

curl -fsSL https://ps1ke.github.io/Baton/install.sh | sh

Linux or macOS, with Python 3.11+ and git. It installs for your user only.

2 · open

Run baton in a terminal

It opens in your browser. The first time, say who you are and choose the models this machine may use.

3 · join

Hand the invite to your baton

With baton running on this computer, this opens it with the invite filled in. Press Join there.
